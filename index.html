The Project

EVAN is an independent multi-instrumentalist and music producer working out of South Carolina. Blending elements across alternative soundscapes, lo-fi melodic arrangements, and bass-heavy industrial electronics, the project represents a refusal to stay locked in a single genre lane.

From the sunny, surf-tinged hooks of early singles to the aggressive synth layers and hard-hitting sample flips found on the 2026 Sapo EP, everything is built from scratch with a heavy focus on production details and distinctive audio textures.
