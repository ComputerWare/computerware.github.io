MY STORY

I’ve always seen music as a technical challenge as much as an emotional one. Growing up in South Carolina, I found myself captivated by the way sound can be manipulated through engineering and software.

I don't just "write" music; I architect it. My process involves taking organic instruments and processing them through a digital lens, creating something that feels both human and futuristic. I believe the future of art lies in this hybrid space.

When I'm not in the studio, I'm usually exploring the latest in reactive hardware or refining my software stack to find new ways to express the complex rhythms of our modern world.
